Leadership

Faithful leadership in a messy world.

Leading others is hard. It’s tiresome, tedious, and oftentimes messy. This is why leadership cannot be neatly summarized by a list of techniques or measured by a set of criteria.

True leaders are defined by faithfulness. They steadfastly serve and guide others through the “muck” of life, ready for tough conversations, and they leave the outcome in the hands of Christ.

Marriages, families, workplaces, and churches need men committed to leading with strength, integrity, and humility, no matter the circumstances.

Our equipping approach

Three things every leader needs.

Character development

At The Forge, leadership development is character development. We equip men to become servant-leaders, to have Christlike character, to model integrity, and to boldly live out their faith.

Faith formation

Group leaders play an important role in faith formation. Leaders have a unique opportunity to foster relationships and guide men toward a life of faith and service, as disciple makers in keeping with the Great Commission.

Biblical counseling principles

Any man coming for advice wants wisdom and care. The course teaches basic biblical counseling principles so leaders can discern the needs of the men in their groups and offer wise counsel in the moment.
