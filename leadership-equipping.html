Faithful Leadership in a Messy World

Leading others is hard. It’s tiresome, tedious, and oftentimes messy. This is why leadership cannot be neatly summarized by a list of techniques or measured according to a set of criteria.

True leaders are defined by faithfulness. They steadfastly serve and guide others through the “muck” of life. They are ready and willing to have tough conversations, yet leave questions about the success or failure of their leadership in the hands of Christ, resting in the knowledge that the Wonderful Counselor and King of Kings will use them as He wills.

Marriages, families, workplaces, and churches need men who are committed to the vision of leading faithfully and steadfastly with strength, integrity, and humility, no matter the circumstances.

Our Equipping Approach

Character Development

At The Forge, leadership development is character development. We strive to build and equip men become servant-leaders, to have Christ-like character, to model integrity, and to boldly live out their faith in society.

Faith Formation

Group leaders play an important role in faith formation. Whether the realize it or not, leaders have a unique opportunity to foster relationships and guide men to live a life of faith and service. At The Forge, leaders are equipped in their role as disciple makers in keeping with the Great Commission.

Biblical Counseling Principles

Any man coming for advice, guidance, or to talk about an issue desires wisdom and care. The Leader Equipping Course teaches basic biblical counseling principles to help leaders discern the needs of men in their respective groups and offer wise counsel according to the moment.
