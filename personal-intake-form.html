Personal Intake

Biblical Counseling provided by The Forge seeks to offer biblical guidance and truth to help men resolve difficulties, ease pain, confront sin, and make wise decisions. The aim of biblical counseling is to reorient incorrect or unhealthy motives, desires, and behaviors toward a life aligned with God's design for man. This is done to restore a right relationship with God and fellowship with others through Jesus Christ. Biblical counselors at The Forge prayerfully guide men toward genuine faith in Jesus Christ and reliance upon the Holy Spirit's power, leading to a heart change necessary for living a Christ-centered life to the full.

Please share the information requested below to aid our counselors in understanding who you are and the concerns you have at this time. Complete the form as thoroughly as possible. All information is confidential (see Counseling Info & Agreement) and privately held by The Forge Counselor assigned to you.
