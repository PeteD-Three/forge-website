A New Mission for Veterans

Rally Point brings veterans together around a new mission built on brotherhood, purpose, and faith. Through a trusted community of veterans who understand the journey, men find a new unit where they can face life's battles together and move forward with strength, connection, and purpose.

Rally Point

A Veteran’s Field Guide to Mental Health and Soul Restoration helps veterans navigate life after military service through personal stories, mental health assessments, and biblical truth, offering hope and a path toward healing.

The Rally Point Curriculum is an 8-week, peer-to-peer journey developed by a veteran, trained counselor, and pastor at heart. Each veteran-led cohort becomes a brotherhood, walking together toward healing, purpose, and soul restoration.

What to Expect

At Rally Point, brotherhood isn't defined by branch of service, war era, rank, or age. Veterans come together around a shared mission: to pursue peace through faith and connection to a community of brothers who understand.

Expect to be challenged, encouraged, and known. This is not a journey veterans are asked to walk alone. Your cohort encourages you to find your new unit through a group of trusted men growing together through study, conversation, prayer, and shared experience.

Biblical Counseling for Veterans

Biblical counseling offers veterans space to work through the challenges of military service and life beyond it.

Rooted in Scripture, compassionate care, and the hope found in Jesus Christ, counseling helps veterans navigate personal struggles, find renewed purpose, and move toward lasting healing and soul restoration.

Become a Rally Point Group Leader

Rally Point Leaders are veterans who feel called to help other veterans find freedom and healing. You will be equipped through The Forge Leader Equipping Course and receive training specific to Rally Point. Classes are delivered online or in person based on need and this certification is completely free of charge to the veteran thanks to the support of our generous donors.

Clay Rojas

Regional Director in Tulsa and USMC Veteran

Email: clayr@theforge.org

The heart behind Rally Point is deeply personal. Clay Rojas, a USMC veteran with three combat deployments, counselor, and author of Rally Point: A Veteran's Field Guide to Mental Health and Soul Restoration, brings together firsthand military experience, years of working with veterans and first responders, and a passion for helping men find restoration in Jesus Christ.

During his service in the United States Marine Corps, Clay led Marines as a Squad Leader with 2nd FAST Company and later as an Infantry Squad Leader with 1st Battalion, 7th Marine Regiment. Today, he serves as The Forge's Regional Director in Tulsa, Oklahoma, using his experience to walk alongside veterans and equip others to do the same.

Through Rally Point and the broader work of The Forge, Clay's heart is to help veterans find trusted brotherhood, wise biblical counsel, and a path toward hope and healing—reminding them that while one mission may have ended, God is not finished writing their story.

Whether you're a veteran seeking hope, a church looking to partner, or someone who believes every veteran deserves authentic brotherhood and biblical hope, you can help make a lasting impact.
