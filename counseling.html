Our approach

What makes it biblical?

We look at your struggle through the lens of Scripture, what the Creator says about human emotions, relationships, and motives. Our counselors are not a licensed counseling agency, but they bring advanced degrees, certifications, and decades of experience in Christian counseling, in churches, and in the business world.

Read the full explanation

At The Forge, we seek to understand the root of psychological distress and delve into our counselees’ fear, trauma, difficult relationships, childhood, and motivations. We provide comfort and care as we explore ways with our counselees to stop harmful behaviors, addictive patterns, and troubling thoughts. We ask pointed questions, identify patterns, and come alongside our counselees to seek solutions to life’s struggles.

The methodology described above aligns with numerous approaches to counseling since the art of asking questions, listening, and drawing conclusions is common to all counseling. So what makes biblical counseling at The Forge “biblical”?

The answer to this question is found by examining the lens used to make observations and draw conclusions. All psychological theories focus on certain techniques to use, questions to ask, and solutions to pursue based on a lens with which the world is viewed. In establishing a specific counseling approach, each theory first assumes answers to fundamental questions about life and the nature of mankind. How a theory answers these questions will unquestionably affect the way counseling is conducted.

  • What motivates people to act the way they do?
  • What is each person’s purpose in life?
  • Who or what defines right and wrong?
  • What are people ultimately responsible for and to whom?
  • What solutions should counseling seek to achieve?

Biblical counseling answers these questions by examining what is communicated by the Creator about human emotions, interactions, and motivations, and by making observations about society that are filtered through the lens of Scripture.

As the co-founder and lead counselor at The Forge, Dr. John Blackburn brings 40 years of pastoral counseling experience to his roles. His mission is to point men to Jesus—guiding them through difficult seasons toward true and lasting health, hope, and wholeness through the professional biblical counseling and compassionate care he provides. Dedicated to bringing clarity to the confused and renewal to the destressed, John specializes in helping men restore broken relationships and heal from deep-seeded wounds, regrets, and sinful habits.

John is a Board-Certified Professional Counselor (BCPC), certified by the American Association of Christian Counselors, and an active member of the Association of Biblical Counselors (ABC). He has earned a Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Biblical Education (1984), a Master of Religious Education (MRE) (1995), and a Doctor of Religious Studies (DRS) with an emphasis in Pastoral Care and Counseling (2021).

As an author, John wrote Keys to a Healthy, Happy, and Holy Marriage: Unlock the Full Potential in Your Relationship (Westbow Press, 2024). He has also authored several booklets published by The Forge addressing vital topics such as prayer, discerning God's will, authentic friendships, passivity, lust, anger, grief, and pride.

On a personal note, John and his wife, Beth, have been happily married since 1982. They are the proud parents of three grown children and are blessed with twelve grandchildren.
