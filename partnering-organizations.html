Leader Equipping Partnerships

The Forge partners with Promise Keepers, Fahrenheit Real Life Mentoring, and Impact Players through our Leader Equipping Course and Lunch & Learn experiences.

Strengthening the Local Church

The Forge believes the local church is the center for care and discipleship.

We partner with churches to equip pastors, ministry teams, group leaders, and everyday believers with biblical counseling principles and practical tools for walking alongside people through life's challenges.

Our goal is simple: help churches cultivate a Christ-centered, burden-bearing culture where people are known, supported, and cared for.

Our Veteran Partners

The Forge is honored to collaborate with organizations committed to the care and well-being of veterans.

Through The Forge Veteran’s Initiative, these relationships strengthen our ability to connect veterans with trusted resources, authentic brotherhood, biblical counsel, and opportunities to rediscover purpose and hope.
