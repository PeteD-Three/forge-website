Privacy

Privacy policy.

How The Forge handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: November 2026. We keep this short and plain. If anything is unclear, contact us and we’ll explain.

What we collect

  • When you ask for counseling: your name, email, phone number, and the choices you make in the request form (type of counseling, topics, location, preferred counselor, and best times).
  • When you join a course or contact us about groups: your name, email, optional phone number, your location, and anything you choose to write.
  • When you sign up for our newsletter: your email address.

We ask only for what we need to follow up. You can share more with your counselor in person.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, match you with a counselor or group, schedule sessions and trainings, and, if you opt in, send news from The Forge. We do not sell your information.

Confidentiality

What you share in counseling and in groups is held in confidence, especially between a counselor and the person they are helping. Your counselor can explain how confidentiality works when you meet.

Who handles it

Form submissions are delivered to The Forge team by a form service that acts on our behalf. Gifts are processed by Planning Center’s Church Center, which has its own privacy policy. This site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. We do not run advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you gave us, or to remove you from our newsletter, at any time. Contact us at info@theforge.org or (913) 214-6914.
