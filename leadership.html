“As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another.” – Proverbs 27:17

The Forge offers biblical counseling to provide hope and help with the difficulties and temptations all men face. We are not a licensed counseling agency, but our biblical counselors have advanced degrees, certifications, and decades of experience in Christian counseling along with years of professional experience in churches and in the business world.
